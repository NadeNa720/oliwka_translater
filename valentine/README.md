# Valentine's Day Website 💕

A simple, romantic page: your photo, the question, and Yes/No buttons.  
**No** runs away when she tries to click it. **Yes** reveals a celebration with your photos.

## How to add your photos

1. **Main photo (on the question card)**  
   Put your favourite photo of you two in this folder and name it:
   - **`our-photo.jpg`**  
   (You can use `.png` too — then in `index.html` change `our-photo.jpg` to `our-photo.png`.)

2. **Photos after she clicks "Yes"**  
   Put 3 more photos in this folder and name them:
   - **`photo1.jpg`**
   - **`photo2.jpg`**
   - **`photo3.jpg`**  

   In `index.html` you can add more photos by copying a `<div class="photo-box">...</div>` block and changing the `src` to your new file name.

## How to open the website

- Double-click **`index.html`** to open it in your browser, or  
- Drag `index.html` into Chrome/Edge/Firefox.

If you use your own photos, keep the `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `script.js` in the same folder as the image files.

## What happens

- **No button:** Moves to a random place on the screen when she hovers or clicks it (so it’s hard to click).
- **Yes button:** Hides the question, shows “Yes! 💕”, your extra photos, and a short message.

You can edit the text in `index.html` (e.g. the question or the “I love you” message) to personalize it.
